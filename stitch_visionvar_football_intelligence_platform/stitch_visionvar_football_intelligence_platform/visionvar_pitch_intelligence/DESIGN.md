## Brand & Style
The design system powers an elite sports telemetry and VAR (Video Assistant Referee) control room environment. The brand personality is uncompromisingly clinical, hyper-focused, and technologically authoritative. Built for broadcast directors, tactical analysts, and certified match officials operating under extreme scrutiny and zero-latency constraints, the interface must evoke complete confidence, optical precision, and instant cognitive clarity.

The visual style merges **Technical Minimalism**, **Broadcast HUD Glassmorphism**, and **Instrument-Grade High-Contrast Telemetry**. The viewport is treated not as a standard enterprise SaaS application, but as a direct digital twin of the pitch—a cinematic, carbon-layered operational cockpit. Dark, light-absorbing obsidian foundations isolate the video feed while neon laser-grade indicators slice through high-density data. Every border, rule, crosshair, and telemetry read-out serves a tactical verification purpose. Unnecessary decoration is stripped away in favor of sub-pixel alignment, hairline borders, and targeted luminescent cues that mirror military avionics and broadcast graphics suites.

## Colors
The color architecture is calibrated exclusively for pitch-black and dim broadcast review suites, prioritizing low eye strain during long matches while providing critical contrast ratios against 4K HDR live streams.

- **Base Canvas & Canvas Tiers**:
  - Deep Pitch Carbon (`#0B0F15`): Root canvas, baseline viewports, and deep-letterbox frames.
  - Graphite Console (`#101622`): Secondary surface for docked timeline rails, tactical control banks, and radar trays.
  - Elevating HUD Glass (`#172133` at 75% opacity with 16px blur): Floating toolbars, metadata chips, and flyout telemetry modules.
  - Hairline Dividers (`#1E293B` and `#334155`): 1px structural boundaries maintaining clinical order.

- **Instrument Accents & Telemetry Roles**:
  - **Electric Emerald (`#00FF88` / `#10B981`)**: Primary tracking confirmation, onside vectors, validated AI player nodes, and positive decision status.
  - **Laser Cyan / Ice Blue (`#00E5FF` / `#38BDF8`)**: Ball trajectory vectors, optical flow velocity cones, dynamic spatial zones, and tactical mini-pitch radar markers.
  - **Review Amber (`#FFB800` / `#F59E0B`)**: Active VAR check status, questionable contact zones, potential handball incidents, and frame sync cautions.
  - **Laser Crimson (`#FF3366` / `#EF4444`)**: 3D calibrated offside lines, foul impact points, red card threshold alerts, and tracking disruption warnings.
  - **Ultra Violet (`#6366F1`)**: Neural confidence metrics, multi-camera pose estimation overlays, and automated event probability telemetry.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy implements a high-functioning dual-engine architecture:
1. **Space Grotesk** commands the operational display roles, major match phases, and primary modal verdicts. Its architectural, technical letterforms bring cutting-edge broadcast character while remaining structurally rigid.
2. **Inter** handles narrative micro-copy, contextual system feedback, referee review logs, and system configuration screens where neutral, uncolored legibility is paramount.
3. **JetBrains Mono** is enforced across all metric overlays, match clock runtimes (`MM:SS:FF`), spatial X/Y/Z coordinate positions, velocity meters (`km/h`), offside spatial margin calculations (`±0.02m`), and camera timecode feeds (`SMPTE`). All numerical data must render using tabular figures (`tnum`) to eliminate micro-jitter during 60/120fps video playback.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a **zero-waste, viewport-locked orchestration**. Because broadcast video operations cannot afford scroll-induced tracking lag, the viewport maximizes video surface area to edge-to-edge geometry.

- **No Permanent Sidebars**: Fixed navigation rails are eliminated. In their place, a primary floating pill HUD is anchored to the top-center, and a multi-track scrub timeline floats docked to the bottom.
- **Micro-Spaced HUD Grid**: Spacing scales are tightly calibrated around 4px and 12px steps to support ultra-dense metadata display without clutter. Padding inside toolbars and control banks remains compact (`space-xs` to `space-md`).
- **Dynamic Workspaces**:
  - **Solo Feed Priority**: Full-width video frame with unobtrusive floating HUD overlays.
  - **Quad / Split-VAR Matrix**: 4-up synchronized camera feeds separated by razor-thin 1px gridlines, auto-collapsing secondary panels to maintain 16:9 aspect ratios.
  - **Telemetry Radar Dock**: Anchored mini tactical radar that collapses to an iconified floating lens or expands to an overlay HUD with `margin` safe zones.
- **Breakpoints**: Desktop-first architecture (1440px+ and 1920px+ control room monitors). On tablet-class viewports (1024px–1366px), floating tools dock into a compact bottom control cluster, while secondary telemetry readouts switch to sliding inspector sheets.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system is achieved without muddy drop-shadows or high-displacement light sources. Instead, depth is structured through **optical luminosity tiers, dark-field translucency, and electromagnetic border glow**:

- **Ground Zero (Pitch Layer)**: Pure `#0B0F15` or raw live feed video frame. Zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Integrated Tool Rails & Timeline Trays)**: `#101622` with a 1px solid `#1E293B` top or lateral boundary.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action HUDs & Quick-Action Pills)**: Background `#172133` at 80% opacity, 18px backdrop-filter blur, 1px perimeter border of `#334155` (alpha 0.6), and a subtle dark-rimmed interior inset (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- **Tier 3 (Active Critical Overlays & Offside Line Verifications)**: Luminescent bounding boxes. Active selections emit a targeted 6px–12px neon halo (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 255, 136, 0.25)` for Emerald or `0 0 16px rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.35)` for Crimson) that directly signals focused match state without obstructing neighboring pixels.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy communicates tactical precision and military-spec avionics. Sharp, disciplined corner geometry reinforces instrument durability:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to video viewport corners, timeline event blocks, data parameter tags, and secondary action triggers.
- **Interactive Controls (0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to HUD inspector panels, modal dialogue boxes, and AI confidence badges.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px)**: Reserved exclusively for top-level broadcast status flags (e.g., `VAR IN PROGRESS`, `GOAL DECISION VALIDATED`), high-level category switchers, and the primary floating navigation bar. This sharp contrast between boxy technical panels and continuous pill markers instantly guides eye movement to system-wide states.

## Components

### 1. Floating Nav & HUD Command Pills
- **Appearance**: Semi-transparent `#172133` pill capsule with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and 1px `#334155` border.
- **Content**: Match clock (`MM:SS:ms`), period indicator (`1H`, `2H`, `ET`), primary review triggers, and quick view toggles.
- **Interactive State**: Hover shifts the border to `#00E5FF` (Laser Cyan) at 40% opacity; active selection triggers an Electric Emerald micro-pip.

### 2. Micro-Scrub Timeline & Multi-Track Channel
- **Layout**: Horizontally stretchable track across the viewport baseline.
- **Channels**: Main Match Video, 4-Camera Sync Markers, Optical Ball Speed Spike Track, and Biometric/Skeletal Tracking keyframe bars.
- **Playhead**: 1px pure `#00FF88` needle with a top coordinate flag rendering exact frame count (`FR: 184,920`) in JetBrains Mono.
- **Event Stamps**: Mini vertical pill pills color-coded by event (Laser Crimson for fouls, Broadcast Amber for reviews, Laser Cyan for set pieces).

### 3. Precision Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary (Verification Action)**: High-visibility solid `#00FF88` background with `#0B0F15` bold typography. On hover: subtle scale, outer halo `0 0 18px rgba(0, 255, 136, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary (HUD Auxiliary)**: `#101622` base with 1px `#334155` border and `#00E5FF` text.
- **Danger / Overturn**: `#FF3366` laser fill or border with crimson ambient glow for irreversible calls (e.g., "Overturn Goal", "Confirm Red Card").

### 4. Telemetry Chips & Confidence Indicators
- **Structure**: Compact 20px–24px height tags with JetBrains Mono numbers.
- **AI Confidence Gauge**: Integrated circular mini-radial or progress micro-bar displaying model confidence percentage (e.g., `SKELETAL CONF: 99.4%` in `#6366F1`).
- **Pitch Coordinates**: `X: 42.12m | Y: -12.40m | Z: 1.18m` rendered with high-contrast neutral borders.

### 5. Tactical Pitch Mini-Radar & Freeze-Frame Tools
- **Radar**: 2D tactical pitch representation rendered with razor-sharp `#1E293B` yard lines, dynamic laser dots showing player centroids with velocity vectors, and a glowing cyan dot for ball tracking.
- **Freeze-Frame Crosshairs**: 1px segmented dotted grid overlay with digital lens magnification loupe (`2x`, `4x`, `8x`) surrounded by a calibrated hairline ring showing sub-millimeter measurements.

### 6. Form Elements & Toggles
- **Inputs & Scrub Sliders**: Stepped numeric inputs with mono digits; sliders use ultra-thin 2px tracks with 10px high-contrast diamond or pill thumbs.
- **Checkboxes & Segmented Radios**: Angular, 4px-radius boxes with sharp neon check marks; selected state illuminates the surrounding container with low-bleed radiance.
